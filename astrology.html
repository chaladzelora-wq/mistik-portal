The Zodiac

The zodiac is a celestial coordinate system used to identify the positions of planets. Divided into 12 signs, each with its own unique characteristics, the zodiac represents the eternal cycle of cosmic energy.

Your Astrological Profile

Every individual is born under a specific sun sign, moon sign, and rising sign. These three elements form the foundation of your astrological identity. The Sun represents your core essence, the Moon your emotional landscape, and the Rising your outward persona.

Planetary Influences

Mercury guides communication, Venus rules love and beauty, Mars drives ambition, and the outer planets Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto orchestrate the larger rhythms of transformation and growth.

The Houses

The 12 houses of the zodiac chart represent different life areas: self, possessions, communication, home, creativity, health, relationships, death and rebirth, philosophy, career, friendships, and spirituality. Each house is governed by a sign and illuminated by planetary placements.
