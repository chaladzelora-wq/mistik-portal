The Ancient Art of Hand Reading

Palmistry, or chiromancy, is an ancient divination practice that interprets the lines, mounts, and shapes of the hands to reveal personality traits, life events, and spiritual destiny. Every hand tells a unique story.

The Major Lines

The Life Line: Begins between thumb and index finger, curves around the base of the thumb. Reveals life vitality and major life changes. The Heart Line: Runs horizontally across the upper palm. Indicates emotional health and love matters. The Head Line: Runs horizontally across the middle palm. Reflects intellectual capacity and decision-making. The Fate Line: Runs vertically from wrist to middle finger. Shows destiny and life direction.

The Mounts

The fleshy elevations on the palm beneath each finger correspond to planetary energies: Jupiter (leadership), Saturn (wisdom), Apollo (creativity), Mercury (communication). Developed mounts indicate strong expression of these qualities.

Hand Dominance and Shape

The dominant hand (right for most) shows conscious energies and current life direction. The non-dominant hand reveals innate potential and past influences. Hand shapes—earth, water, fire, air—correspond to personality types and how you engage with the world.
