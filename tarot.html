The Major Arcana

The Major Arcana consists of 22 cards that represent the great archetypal forces of the universe. From The Fool at the beginning to The World at the end, these cards chronicle the human journey of spiritual development and self-discovery.

The Minor Arcana

Divided into four suits—Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles—the Minor Arcana reflects the everyday challenges and joys of human existence. Each suit corresponds to an element and aspect of life: creativity, emotion, intellect, and material prosperity.

Reading the Cards

Tarot reading is an ancient art of divination. The position of cards, their orientation (upright or reversed), and their interplay within a spread all contribute to the message the cards wish to convey. Common spreads include the Three-Card Spread, the Celtic Cross, and the Wheel of the Year.

Reversed Cards

When a card appears upside down, it often indicates an internalized, blocked, or shadow aspect of the card's meaning. Reversed cards are not inherently negative—they invite deeper reflection and hidden truths.
